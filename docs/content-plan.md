# Learn article plan

Two to three articles a week, each checked against the site's own food data before
publishing. Goal: a stronger site for the AdSense re-review in early December 2026.

**How each article is made:** Claude drafts it in `src/data/learn/<slug>.ts` with every
number calculated from `src/data/foods` (scripts in the session, not guessed). You read it,
add a line or two from your own experience, then it is committed and deployed.

**Rules:** no topic that repeats an existing article; every article links to at least one
tool; numbers come from the site's data, and outside figures (NHS, USDA, FDA) are named.
